# Business Insights 360 | Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive view of business performance across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive functions.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to analyze key business metrics, identify performance trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyze Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit
- Compare performance against benchmarks and previous years
- Identify high and low-performing products and customers
- Analyze regional and market-level performance
- Evaluate supply chain and forecast accuracy
- Provide actionable insights through interactive dashboards

## 📌 Dashboard Views

- Regional and market performance
